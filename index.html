How this scorer reads the guide

  • Minimum 8 points. Flowers do not count toward it. Declaring Mahjong below 8 costs 10 points to each player.
  • Two Concealed Kongs scores 8. The guide lists it in the 8-point section but shows 6 in its summary table; 8 matches the official rules.
  • Reversible Tiles uses Dots 1-2-3-4-5-8-9 and Bamboo 2-4-5-6-8-9. The guide swaps the two suits, which would include tiles that are not symmetrical.
  • Dragon and Wind pungs that score Dragon Pung, Prevalent Wind or Seat Wind do not also score Pung of Terminals or Honors.
  • Pairs of chows (Pure Double Chow, Short Straight and similar) are each counted once, and a chow joins at most two such pairs.
  • Seven Pairs, Thirteen Orphans and Knitted hands drop Concealed Hand and Single Wait as the guide says, but still score Fully Concealed Hand when self-drawn.
